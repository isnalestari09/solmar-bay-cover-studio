Solmar Bay Cover Studio

Turn your photo and a caption into GTA-style cover art — offline, in your browser.

1 · Size

2 · Panels

Three diagonal panels. Pick a drawn scene for each, or drop in your own photo — drag the cover to move a photo, use the zoom slider to frame it.

3 · Words

4 · Colour grade

Live preview — click a panel to select it. Export downloads a full-resolution PNG. Press E to export, R for a random cover.

Solmar Bay is a fictional city. All art is drawn in code by this app — no photos are uploaded anywhere, and no licensed artwork is used.
